## Non-negotiables for EASY SCAN

- **Landscape first.** Terrain is the largest, most important element. Choose land over UI.
- **Three fonts, three jobs.** ABNES = identity (short text only) · TacticSans = structure · Andale Mono = information, body copy, every number.
- **Black ground, white ink.** Coral `#FF8562` is the brand accent; scan-green `#3DE3A6` is the instrument accent. No third accent.
- **Data colour carries meaning** and must never be the only carrier — always colour + line style + label + symbol.
- **Never fabricate spatial data.** Label illustrative figures `DEMO DATA`; never present an approximate boundary as legally verified or conceptual architecture as existing.
- **No emoji. No neon, HUD or cyberpunk effects. No glassmorphism. No rainbow elevation ramps.**
