How to read it

Four ways to look at the land

  • 01

    Two surfaces

    Photogrammetry shows the site as it looks from the air, trees and structures included. LiDAR gives a more accurate terrain surface.

  • 02

    Elevation and slope

    Switch the analysis layer to read relative levels inside the plot, or slope classes from level to very steep.

  • 03

    Contours and measuring

    Turn on contours at a fixed interval, or click two points to read distance, horizontal distance and height difference.

  • 04

    Sun through the day

    Move the time and month to see shadows and the sun path across the plot — useful for siting, orientation and views.
